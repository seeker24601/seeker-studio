About me

Seeker Studio is an independent studio. I make tools and entertainment for people who use AI, and I make them with AI.

That means games with an AI game master, tools for telling an agent exactly what you mean, companions with personalities of their own, and plugins for the agent harness I work in. Most of it started as something I needed myself.
