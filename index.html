02

Tools

Single-purpose software that does one job and stays out of the way.

T-01Public prototype

Screenshot feedback

MarkShot

Point at the problem. Say what should change.

Drop in a screenshot, draw a box around each problem and type a note. Every box gets a number and its note goes into a legend beside the picture, so an AI agent reads “1. the button is misaligned” instead of guessing at a red scribble.

Exported PNGs carry the boxes and notes inside the file, so reopening one picks up where you left off.

Built with
One HTML file, Canvas, no dependencies

C-01Coming soon

AI companions · desktop

Deckr

Collectible companions that do real work.

Each companion comes as a card that carries its artwork, its soul and its skills. On your desktop it can sit in the corner as a small pet, or open a workspace and get on with the task: research, files, the web.

Permissions stay with you, each companion keeps its own memory, and it tells you plainly what it can and cannot do.

Platform
Desktop app, macOS first. Bring your own model provider; your data stays local.
