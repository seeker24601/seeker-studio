T-01Public prototype

Screenshot feedback

MarkShot

Point at the problem. Say what should change.

Drop in a screenshot, draw a box around each problem and type a note. Every box gets a number and its note goes into a legend beside the picture, so an AI agent reads “1. the button is misaligned” instead of guessing at a red scribble.

Exported PNGs carry the boxes and notes inside the file, so reopening one picks up where you left off.

Built with
One HTML file, Canvas, no dependencies

T-03In development

Writing cleanup

Deslopifier

Find and rewrite recurring AI writing patterns.

Paste a draft to flag stock phrases, vague descriptions and formulaic sentence structures in context. Deslopifier rewrites the affected paragraphs while checking for changes to meaning and tone.

Rewrites go through checks for retained or newly introduced patterns. When an edit fails verification, the original paragraph stays and a review note explains why.

Format
Web editor and API
T-04Download · v0.1.1

Live fact checking · macOS

Ackshually...

A fact-check that pops up while you're still talking.

Ackshually... sits in the menu bar and listens to a Slack huddle, a YouTube video, or any app playing audio, then transcribes it on your Mac.

Each claim it catches gets checked against your own notes, such as an Obsidian vault, or the public web. When a claim and a source disagree, a small toast shows the correction and where it came from.

It can offer to start on its own when a Slack call begins. Bring your own OpenRouter or OpenAI key; raw audio never leaves the Mac.

Platform
Apple silicon Mac · macOS 15 or later

Early prototype, not notarized by Apple. Accuracy hasn't been benchmarked.

AI companions · desktop

Deckr

Collectible companions that do real work.

Each companion comes as a card that carries its artwork, its soul and its skills. On your desktop it can sit in the corner as a small pet, or open a workspace and get on with the task: research, files, the web.

Permissions stay with you, each companion keeps its own memory, and it tells you plainly what it can and cannot do.

Platform
Desktop app, macOS first. Bring your own model provider; your data stays local.
